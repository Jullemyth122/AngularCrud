Demonstrating Angular Input/Output (React Props & Callbacks) equivalents

Props & Callbacks (React: props → Angular: input & output)

In React, you pass variables down as props and pass functions down as callback props: <ContactForm title="Get In Touch" onSubmit={handleCallback} />.

In Angular, we use property binding [headerTitle]="'Get In Touch With Me'" to pass inputs, and event binding (formSubmit)="onFormSubmitted($event)" to receive callback events.
